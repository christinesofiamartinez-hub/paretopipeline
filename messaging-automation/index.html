WHAT WE CAN AUTOMATE

Every conversation can have a next step.

We build messaging automations around the moments that matter in your customer’s journey—from discovering your offer to enquiring, booking, or buying.

01Capture Leads

Comment-to-DM Lead Generation

Turn social media engagement into private conversations.

When someone comments on eligible content using an agreed trigger, they can receive a relevant private message. That message can share more information about your offer, deliver a resource, collect details, or guide them toward the next step.

HOW IT WORKSYou publish a post promoting your service or offer. Someone comments with the agreed keyword, receives a private message, and can explore your offer or follow a link to enquire.
THE BENEFITCreate a clearer path from engaging with your content to expressing interest in your business.
02Capture Leads

Lead Capture & Qualification

Find out who’s interested and what they’re looking for.

We build guided conversations that ask relevant questions and collect information from potential customers, such as their needs, service preferences, or contact details. Where supported, responses can be organized or passed to compatible business tools.

HOW IT WORKSSomeone enquires about working with you. The conversation asks what they’re looking for, gathers the necessary details, and directs them toward a relevant service or consultation.
THE BENEFITGet more organized inquiries and spend less time manually collecting the same information.
03Convert Prospects

DM-to-Booking Automation

Make it easier for potential customers to book.

We create messaging flows that explain your services, answer common questions, and guide interested prospects toward your booking page or consultation process.

HOW IT WORKSSomeone asks about your services. They explore relevant options, get answers to common questions, and follow a booking link when they’re ready.
THE BENEFITGive prospects a clearer path to booking with less unnecessary back-and-forth.
04Convert Prospects

Sales & Offer Conversion Flows

Help interested prospects understand your offer and take action.

We build conversations that present your offers, explain what’s included, answer common objections, and direct potential customers to the appropriate checkout, application, or inquiry page.

HOW IT WORKSSomeone asks about a service or product. The conversation walks them through the relevant information and gives them a clear next step to purchase or enquire.
THE BENEFITMake your offer easier to explore so interested prospects know what to do next.
05Convert Prospects

Lead Follow-Up & Re-Engagement

Give interested prospects a reason to continue the conversation.

Some people need more time before making a decision. Where messaging permissions and platform rules allow, we can set up relevant follow-ups for prospects who have requested information or shown interest but haven’t continued.

HOW IT WORKSSomeone enquires about your offer but doesn’t book. An appropriate follow-up can remind them about the information they requested and provide a way to continue their inquiry.
THE BENEFITCreate a more consistent follow-up process instead of relying entirely on memory and manual reminders.
06Grow Customer Value

Lead Nurturing

Help potential customers make a more informed decision.

We build messaging sequences that share useful information, explain your services, answer common questions, and introduce relevant next steps over time.

HOW IT WORKSSomeone requests a free resource but isn’t ready to buy. They can receive an appropriate sequence of helpful messages that introduces your offer and explains how to learn more.
THE BENEFITGive interested prospects more opportunities to understand your offer before deciding whether to buy.
07Convert Prospects

Abandoned Booking & Inquiry Recovery

Help prospects return to an unfinished next step.

Where the necessary tracking, integrations, and permissions are available, we can explore ways to follow up with people who started a booking or inquiry process but didn’t complete it.

HOW IT WORKSSomeone begins an inquiry or booking process but leaves before finishing. An eligible follow-up can direct them back to the relevant page or offer help with the next step.
THE BENEFITCreate a way to reconnect with some prospects who may otherwise leave the process unfinished.
08Grow Customer Value

Upsell & Repeat-Purchase Opportunities

Help existing customers discover what else you offer.

For businesses with suitable complementary services or repeat-purchase opportunities, we can create messaging flows that introduce relevant offers to eligible customers.

HOW IT WORKSAfter a customer purchases a service, an appropriate follow-up can introduce a complementary offer or invite them to book their next appointment.
THE BENEFITMake relevant next purchases easier to discover without manually contacting every customer.

You don’t need every automation on this list. We’ll learn about your offer, your customer journey, and the opportunities you’d like to explore, then recommend a practical starting point.

FAQS

Frequently asked questions

What exactly does Pareto Pipeline do?

We build and manage revenue-focused messaging automations that can help businesses capture leads, qualify inquiries, guide prospects toward bookings or purchases, follow up appropriately, and nurture potential customers.

Can this help my business generate more sales?

These automations can make it easier for interested people to take the next step and help you manage inquiries more consistently. Actual sales depend on factors such as your offer, audience, pricing, traffic, and sales process. We don’t guarantee a particular revenue result.

Do I need a large audience to benefit?

Not necessarily. The right setup depends on how people discover your business, how they enquire, and what you want them to do next. We’ll discuss whether messaging automation makes sense for your current situation.

Do you only work on new setups?

No. We can discuss an existing setup and assess whether it can be improved, expanded, or managed through one of our packages.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. We handle the agreed setup and ongoing management. You’ll provide the necessary access, information, content, and approvals, and we’ll guide you through what’s needed.

Can you connect my booking system, CRM, or other tools?

Potentially. It depends on the software, available integrations, permissions, and complexity. We’ll check what’s feasible before confirming what is included.

Can you guarantee more leads, bookings, or sales?

No. We can commit to the agreed work and help monitor relevant indicators, but we can’t control audience behavior, demand, pricing, traffic, or every part of your sales process.

What happens after launch?

Depending on your package, we can maintain your automations, troubleshoot issues, set up campaigns, review available performance data, and implement agreed improvements.

Are subscriptions and software fees included?

No. Software subscriptions, third-party service fees, and advertising spend are separate unless explicitly agreed otherwise.

What do you need from me?

You’ll need to provide the necessary account access, information about your business and offers, approved messaging content, and timely feedback or approvals.

How do I get started?

Book a free discovery call. We’ll learn about your business and discuss where revenue-focused messaging automation might fit. If we’re a good fit, we’ll recommend a suitable package and agree on the next steps.
